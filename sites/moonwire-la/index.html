The Experience

Built for the dance floor

A 1920s textile warehouse, rebuilt from the floor up around sound, light and the people on the floor.

The Vault: Main Room

Raw concrete, 28-foot ceilings and a sunken floor that puts the DJ at eye level. Capacity for 900, room to move for every one of them.

The Sound

A custom-tuned, four-point system with 60,000 watts and stacked subs you feel in your chest, not your ears. Clean at every volume, mapped to every corner.

The Terrace: Rooftop

Downtown skyline views, a full cocktail bar and golden hour every Sunday. Our Sunset Sessions run from 4 PM to midnight.

The Light Show

A kinetic LED ceiling, laser arrays and haze, programmed live by our resident lighting designer to every drop.

FAQ

Before you go

How old do I have to be?

MOONWIRE is strictly 21+. Every guest needs a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID or passport). Digital IDs and photos of IDs are not accepted.

What's the dress code?

Upscale nightlife. Think sharp, not stiff: fitted jeans or trousers, clean sneakers or dress shoes, going-out tops. No athletic wear, sports jerseys, flip-flops or baggy clothing. Sunday rooftop sessions are smart-casual.

Where do I park?

Paid lots are available on E 7th Pl and Mateo St, typically $15–$25 per night. Street parking is limited, so check posted signs carefully. Valet is included with The Elevated and Moon Table packages.

Where should my rideshare drop me off?

Set your Uber or Lyft pickup and drop-off to 1100 E 7th Pl. There is a dedicated rideshare lane in front of the main entrance, and staff will help you find your car at closing.

What's your entry policy?

Advance ticket holders and VIP tables get priority. General admission is first come, first served while capacity allows. Management reserves the right to refuse entry. All guests pass through a security check; no outside drinks, large bags or professional cameras.

Can I get a refund on tickets?

Tickets are non-refundable unless an event is canceled. If a headliner changes, we'll post an update and honor refunds requested before the event starts.

Is there a coat check?

Yes. Coat and bag check is available at the entrance for $5 per item, cash or card.
