Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/30/2026

MOONWIRE ("we," "us") operates the nightclub at 1100 E 7th Pl, Los Angeles, CA 90021 and this website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit, such as your name, email address and mobile phone number.
  • Reservation details, such as event date, party size, package and special requests.
  • Basic technical data from your browser, such as device type and pages visited.
  • Ticket purchases are processed by our third-party ticketing partner under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage VIP table and private event requests.
  • To send guest list emails, presale offers and event updates you signed up for.
  • To send reservation-related calls or text messages if you gave consent.
  • To improve our website and guest experience.

Text messages

  • We only text you if you opt in. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
  • We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing.

Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share data only with service providers that help us run the business (form processing, email, ticketing), and when required by law.

Your choices & California rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message.
  • California residents may request access to or deletion of their personal information under the CCPA/CPRA.
  • To make a request, email vip@moonwire.example or call (213) 555-0187.

Age

  • Our venue and website are intended for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
